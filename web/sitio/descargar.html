AulaPro

AulaPro runs in your browser.

There is nothing to download: AulaPro is used at aulapro.app/app from any computer, tablet or phone, on Windows, Mac, Android, iPhone, iPad or Chromebook.

To get started

  1. Open aulapro.app/app in your browser.
  2. Sign in with your Google or Microsoft account. If you bought AulaPro with that email, the licence activates by itself.
  3. If you like, add it to your home screen or install it from the browser: it opens like an app and works offline.
Open AulaPro

Bought with another email or have a pack? Enter your licence key in the app itself. Used the Windows or Android app? Download your data in Settings › Data and backups and load it in the web version. Any questions, at contacto@aulapro.app.
